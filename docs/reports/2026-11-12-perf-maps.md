# Step 8, PR 4: Aurora's minimap and world map (2026-11-12)

Branch `feat/perf-maps`, stacked on `feat/perf-atlas` ([ADR 0009](../adr/0009-client-optimizations-aurora-owns.md)). The owner asked for three Xaero's Minimap and World Map optimizations, applied to Aurora's own maps instead. Each was measured first; one was worth shipping.

## Minimap Refresh Cap (shipped)

| Gate | UI |
|---|---|
| `perfMinimapRefreshCap` | Settings → Performance → Maps |

How it works:

- A still camera already cost nothing: no repaint, and the grid resamples at 20 Hz at most.
- A moving or turning camera repainted the whole terrain texture on every frame: about 147k device texels plus an upload at the default size.
- With the cap, motion alone repaints at most 60 times a second. Grid or config changes still repaint at once.
- Frames between repaints draw waypoints, entity dots, the arrow and the compass at the last painted pose, so they stay aligned with the terrain under them.
- On screens faster than 60 Hz the terrain scrolls in visible steps. That is a visible trade-off, hence the switch.

Evidence: 26.3 OpenGL, unlocked frame rate, minimap on and rotating with the player, the camera turned 6° per tick, two boots each way:

| | Cap on | Cap off |
|---|---|---|
| `MinimapModule.renderContent`, share of render-thread samples | 2.8%, 2.5% | 9.1%, 9.2% |
| `UiLayerCache.writeAffineAbgr` | 1.0%, 0.6% | 6.3%, 6.3% |
| Mean FPS over 8 s | 573, 580 | 527, 518 |

Visual checks:

- Mid-turn screenshots on 26.3 and 26.2 show the compass letters and terrain rotated together.
- 26.2 and 26.1.2 OpenGL boots applied every mixin with no Aurora warning or error.
- The Performance page shows the new Maps group.

## Measured and dropped

- **World map region throttling** (from Xaero's World Map). With the world map open over captured terrain, all of `WorldMapScreen.drawTerrain` was 0.2% of render-thread samples, and region acquisition (`acquireForRender`, `acquireOverviewForRender`) 0%. Uploads are already budgeted per tick and `uploadPending` already acts as a dirty flag, so checking regions every 8 frames has nothing to save.
- **Radar icon caching** (from Xaero's Minimap). The marker templates are already cached (`MapMarkers`), and the entity list already refreshes at 10 Hz. With 200 mobs in range, the whole minimap was 1.7% of render-thread samples, and the per-entity colour choice a small part of that.
- **Biome caption memo** (minor idea from the plan). `Level.getBiome` took no samples in the minimap runs.

## Tooling

- The probe gained `minimap`, `spin`, `world-map`, `screen` and `cfg <field> <value>`.
- `build/perf-jfr-share2.py` reads JFR text output in about a second, where the JSON reader took minutes.
- Both maps ship off in the release defaults (`minimapEnabled`, `worldMapEnabled`), so `minimap on` enables both for measurement.
