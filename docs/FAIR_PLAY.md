# Fair play

This page is for server owners and for players who want to know what Aurora Client does. An automated test checks it against the code, so it cannot drift from what ships. Edit the notes in `FeatureCatalog.java` and regenerate with `python tools/gen_fair_play_doc.py`; do not edit this file by hand.

## What Aurora never does

- It sends no custom packets and registers no plugin channels.
- It does not change the client's tick rate or the timing of anything sent to the server.
- It collects and sends no telemetry.

The whole mod has two places that send anything to a server. Both are in Better Hitreg, on keys that are unbound until the player binds them: Switch Hand changes the vanilla Main Hand option (the same packet as changing it in Options), and the practice scoreboard's Send key posts the score as an ordinary chat message.

## Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cosmetic | Changes only how things look or sound on your own screen. |
| Vanilla equivalent | Shows your own state or information the vanilla game already presents, or extends a setting or tool vanilla already has. |
| Information advantage | Shows something about other players or the world that vanilla does not present, or allows a view vanilla does not. Ships off. |
| Local timing | Changes when something happens on your own screen. What the game sends to the server, and when, is unchanged. |

## Off in a fresh install

A new install has these off. A player has to turn each one on.

- FreeLook
- Entity Health, which shows the health of mobs and players
- Other players' totem pop counts, on the HUD and on nametags
- Dots for other players on the minimap
- Water and lava clarity (Pack Tweaks)
- Better Hitreg, and inside it the reach rings, extra hitboxes and hiding of other fights

Changing a default does not change an existing install: a player who already had one of these on keeps it on.

## On in a fresh install, and worth knowing

- Full Bright raises brightness past the vanilla maximum.
- No Fog pushes distance fog back.
- Hitboxes are drawn, as vanilla's F3+B draws them.

## Every feature

| Feature | Class | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Zoom | Vanilla equivalent | Narrows the field of view while a key is held, as the vanilla spyglass does. |
| Full Bright | Vanilla equivalent | Extends the vanilla Brightness setting past its maximum, so unlit areas are visible. On in a fresh install. |
| No Fog | Vanilla equivalent | Pushes distance fog back, so terrain inside your render distance is visible sooner than in vanilla. On in a fresh install. |
| Pack Tweaks | Cosmetic | Totem particle size, first-person shield position and a tint for your own shield's cooldown. Its water and lava clarity switches improve visibility inside fluids and ship off. |
| Entity Health | Information advantage | Shows the health of nearby mobs and players above them. Vanilla does not show other players' health. |
| FreeLook | Information advantage | Lets the camera look around without turning the character, a view vanilla does not allow. |
| Saturation Bar | Vanilla equivalent | Shows your own saturation on the food bar. |
| Block Overlay | Cosmetic | Restyles the outline of the block you are looking at. |
| Toggle Sprint/Sneak | Vanilla equivalent | Press-once sprint and sneak, as vanilla's accessibility options offer. |
| Alerts | Vanilla equivalent | Warns about your own armor durability and your own expiring effects. |
| Crosshair | Cosmetic | Replaces the crosshair's shape and color. |
| Hitbox | Vanilla equivalent | Draws entity hitboxes, as vanilla's F3+B does, with custom colors. |
| Better Hitreg | Local timing | Plays your hit's sound, hurt animation and particles on your screen at once instead of waiting for the server's copy. It does not change what the game sends for movement or combat, or when. Two optional keys, unbound by default, send something when pressed: Switch Hand changes the vanilla Main Hand option, and the practice scoreboard's Send key posts the score as a chat message. The feature is off in a fresh install, and so are its reach rings, extra hitboxes and hiding of other fights. |
| Hit Color | Cosmetic | Recolors the flash on an entity that takes damage. |
| Glint Color | Cosmetic | Recolors the enchantment glint. |
| Info HUD | Vanilla equivalent | Shows FPS, coordinates, time, biome and similar readouts that the F3 screen also has. |
| CPS | Vanilla equivalent | Counts your own clicks. |
| Armor HUD | Vanilla equivalent | Shows your own armor and its durability. |
| Reach Display | Vanilla equivalent | Shows the distance of your own last hit. |
| Potion HUD | Vanilla equivalent | Lists your own active effects, as the vanilla effect icons do. |
| Ping | Vanilla equivalent | Shows latency as numbers: your own, and other players' as the tab list already reports it. |
| Totem Pop Counter | Vanilla equivalent | Counts your own totem pops. Counting other players' pops, on the HUD or on their nametags, is an information advantage and ships off. |
| Waypoints | Vanilla equivalent | Marks positions you chose, and where you died, from your own coordinates. |
| Container Preview | Vanilla equivalent | Shows a shulker box's contents as a grid instead of vanilla's text list, and your ender chest as you last saw it. |
| Item Physics | Cosmetic | Changes how dropped items lie and tumble on your screen. |
| Particles | Cosmetic | Hides, resizes or recolors particle types on your screen. |
| Item Scale | Cosmetic | Changes the size and position of the item in your hand. |
| Held Item Seam Fix | Cosmetic | Hides texture seams on the item in your hand. |
| Animations | Cosmetic | Changes swing, view-bob and damage-tilt animations and smooths how other entities move on your screen. |
| Keystrokes | Vanilla equivalent | Shows your own key and mouse presses. |
| Minimap | Vanilla equivalent | A map of terrain your client has loaded, with your waypoints and dots for nearby mobs. Dots for other players are an information advantage and ship off. |
| World Map | Vanilla equivalent | A full-screen map of terrain you have already visited. |
| Resourcepack Browser | Cosmetic | Downloads resource packs from Modrinth. |
| Display Settings | Local timing | Frame pacing, a frame cap, vsync control and the point within a frame where mouse movement is applied. The rate and content of what is sent to the server are unchanged. |
| Custom Title | Cosmetic | Aurora's title screen and menu styling. |
| Text & Fonts | Cosmetic | Changes the font. |
| Theme | Cosmetic | Changes the colors of Aurora's own screens. |
| Interface | Cosmetic | Limits the frame rate of Aurora's own screens. |
